*1507.10.07*  
*A lecture at New Olamn College in Waterdeep, by Adjunct Professor Titan, on the nearly unknown **Netherese Civil War**.*

“In my research on Ammarindar, I was able to uncover some rather unique histories of the **Netheril Empire**. It appears as if the Netherese attempted to remove all traces of this story from their history, the betrayal being so outrageous, they wished no one to even know it happened. Much of the story happened in what is now refered to as the North Sword Coast, or Savage Frontier.

In the late -2300s a Netherese Tiefling named **Karavakos** discovered a rift which allowed a wizard of his abilities nearly effortless travel between Toril and the Fey and Shadow realms. Karavakos used the rift regularly and discovered much of the Shadow realm and the Feywild. He spent many years traveling among these realms and discovering their secrets and their peoples. His writings were some of the first Netherese documentation of those planes. Karavakos was celebrated among the Mages of Netheril. But fame was not nearly enough for the arcanist. He desired to rule his peers, and perhaps all of Toril, or even further as well. 

Karavakos was devoted to, and then and corrupted by Vecna. The Whipered One gave his chosen many gifts, including unnaturally long life, immunity to Netherese magic, and powerful influence over Demons. Karavakos used the latter gift in a way that astounded his contemporaries: he bound the Demon Lord Orcus to his will.

Karavakos demanded an army of Demons and undead from the Blood Lord, which entered Toril through the rift from the Shadowfell in -2210, and which he used to conquer nearly half of low Netheril. The Archmages, relatively secure in their skycities, did not immediately consider this to be a threat to themselves, and largely ignored their peer's exertions. Unknown to the Netheril Empire at the time, because Karavakos was so greedy that even his already long life felt insufficient to him, part of the deal between Karvakos and Orcus was a promise that Karavakos would never die, as long as his Demonic army was undefeated. At the same time, Karavakos used the influence granted by Vecna to make another deal, this time with Yeenoghu, who promised that the army would never be defeated by any mortal force. In return Karavakos promised his army would give no quarter to anyone it faced. It would be an army of extinction, not merely conquest.

Once the Archwizards noticed that Karavakos was not merely conquering and enslaving the low Netherese, but exterminating them (and by extension the Archwizards' source of supplies), they began an organized resistance. It did not go well for the Arcanists. Thanks to the Demonic deals Karavakos had made, the best the Netherese could manage was to slow down the advance of Karavakos' Army. They battled for three years, with the Demonic and Undead hordes making steady progress all the time. Though the Netherese would later attribute the desertification of their lands to the Phaerimm, it actually began in these years.

Long before this, in his years of exploration, Karavakos spent as much time in the Feywild as he did in the Shadowfell. He met and fell in love with an Eladrin Princess named **Vyrellis**. Vyrellis was second daughter of an Eladrin King whose name has not been recorded in the records we have recovered so far. Vyrellis was a Winter Eladrin, and apparently jealous of her older sister and father specifically, and the **Seelie Court** in general. She desired to rule the Feywilds and usurp the Seelie Court, and demanded her lover Karavakos turn his unbeatable army to this task. Karavakos for his part appears to have both discounted any threat his Nentherese peers could muster, and had become lustful for more conquests. He brought his army of demons into the Feywild, believing conquest inevitable. However, he should have been more cognizant of the terms of his deals. Yeenoghu's promise was kept, but the Eladrin forces he now faced were no mortals. The Horde was decimated in the Feywild by the forces of the Seelie Court, and Netherese wizards joined the fray just in time to magically block the demons' retreat back to Toril or the Shadowfell. 

Seeing his army crushed, Karavakos knew all his Demonic machinations were about to come unravelled, his immortality was tied to his army's invincibility, and its invincibility was dependent on his generalship. He murdered his lover in a fit of rage at his inevitable demise, taking her head in a single slash of his demonically cursed blade, raging that she ensnared him, that his defeat was her plan all along, though of course she was not privy to the details of his arrangements with Demon Princes.

Karavakos fell to the Seelie army, and his soul trapped in an extradimensonal prison designed and built by Netherese wizards and Fey powers working together outside of time itself. This was -2207, the year of the birth of the Chronomancer Jeriah Chronos, and the beginning of the Golden Age of Netheril.

Shortly thereafter Netherese mages were able to block the rift, and the empire created a watchtower to ensure that no one would ever be able to reopen it in the future. Of course with the fall of the Netheril Empire after -339 the tower must have been abandoned, and the rift is now faithfully protected by anonymity.
