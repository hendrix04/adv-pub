#VolosGuide to Swordmount
The County of Swordmount was founded in 1494, at the same time the Duchy of Leilon was created by Duke Tobias Wavesilver, a hereditary noble of Waterdeep. Lord Wavesilver had purchased lapsed titles and lands along the northern Sword Coast, and after a short but bloody conflict with fellow Waterdavian nobles the Margasters, and a trial for treason that was subverted by demonic influence, he crowned himself Duke of a dangerous track between Neverwinter and Waterdeep. The existing powers acquiesced to this because the Duchy stabilized the borders between the two City States, though it caused some turmoil further around the Sword Coast.
Duke Wavesilver invested the County to a dragonborn barbarian/adventurer named Caine, an outcast from the Grey Wolf Tribe who was first made Baronette, and a few yers later elevated to Viscount, making him a peer with his neighboring vassal, a green dragon named Claugiyliamatar who, for inscrutable reasons known only to herself, agreed to become a vassal of a human noble.

Swordmount was almost nothing when Caine ascended. The entire population consisted of a score of families living in a logging village, a handful of Druids, and a minor tribe of Elk barbarians who lived there seasonally. The new lord began a successful program to attract immigrants by offering homesteads to new arrivals, and sanctuary to many refugees. He also made a peace between local dwarves and goblins who mined the rich ores beneath his lands, going so far as to make the goblins his constabulary.
