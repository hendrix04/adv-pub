#Beic #Gods #UCR #Pantheon

**Beic,** God of Merchants, exploration, trade, profit, slavery, and exploitation.

**Lord AO,** the Overgod of the Realms, has always been flexible with his Pantheon. Mortals have ascended, as have demons and devils, and Gods have been killed, or been subsumed into their peers. Notably, he has invited Gods of other Pantheons to fulfill their portfolio in the Realms as well as in their home spheres. As mortal theologians began to craft the idea of a Unified Church, it was Lord AO who guided their thoughts. 

Beic is a new god to the Realms though he (or it) is the dominant divine force in his home sphere, without overtly even being divine creature there. He is rarely anthropomorphized at home, instead referred to a sort of 'natural law'. Theologians have rendered his name with some diversity; **Beic, Eric, Ric, Voc.** A few have even suggested **John.** **“The Invisible Hand”** is almost universally agreed upon as an aptronym, though **“The Head”** (in the sense of first, most prominent, most important, as well as the biological sense) is also noted.

Beic is often considered a good god in his home sphere, with some going so far as to call him “the greatest force for good in the universe”, and as a god of merchants, explorers, and traders one can see that point of view. Somewhat more common is the view that Beic 'is just what he is'. A fundamental force of the universe, and neither a being of good or ill. Lastly, in his home sphere there is a repressed minority who revile Beic for the slavery and exploitation that almost always follow where his acolytes tread.

In the Realms, Beic fills the role of the Neutral Evil divine being, along with the God-Lich **Vecna**. His portfolio is diverse, and it is tempting to say that he is more Neutral than Neutral Evil. And unlike some other powers Beic rarely seems passionate about doing evil, he is not seen as encouraging cruelty for its own sake, but rather accepting of cruelty as a consequence of his other imperatives. When anthropomorphized in the realms, Beic is most often depicted as a clerk at desk with an infinite stack of papers he is working on for eternity. A depiction that utilizes lenticular painting to show Beic as a young man, and then aging to a venerable one, all the while sitting at his desk with his unending paperwork has become the standard in UCR temples. Another popular version has Beic as a handsome merchant of Rubinesque porportions, arms open wide and an infectious smile. Far less commonly, Beic is shown as a darkly malevolent shape with a whip, driving slaves ever onward.
