#Theology #Pantheon

It feels worthwhile to reiterate that the Pantheon of the Realms is fundamentally different that it was in the last century, or at any point in the history of Abeir-Toril. The old religion was a collection of divine beings who were a diverse community of friends and enemies, collaborators and competitors, who had vastly different levels of power, divergent interests, and intentions. With no one's status ever being assured, there was much intrigue and backstabbing among the divine at all levels. Lord AO, the overgod, had absolute power over all entities in the Realms, but rarely bothered themself with the actions of the deities and almost never thought about mortals at all.

The new Pantheon is a close group. Seventeen members with nearly equal power, and while some may have aspirations of a greater share of power, the balance that exists largely prevents such. Vecna sits in a Neutral Evil seat. But he is *the* God of new magic. If a wizard wishes to create a new spell, its not Mystra or Isis to whom they must look, but Vecna. No matter if they are good, lawful, chaotic or evil themselves - new magic is funneled exclusively through Vecna. If a Dwarf wants to win a battle, they beseech Gruumush, not Moradin. If a Halfling wants to steal a gem it is Loki, not Yolonda they turn to. If a one seeks justice, be they drow or Yuan-ti, they seek Bahamut.

This consolidated the portfolios of hundreds of gods down to less than a score. Ties to racial deities were frayed in many places, though the old gods do live on as Celestial or Fiendish entities. For example Surtur, former God of fire giants, now is an Angelic creature and vassal of Loki. Fire giants still worship him, as a racial creator, but his domains were ceded to others, the Forge to Moradin, War to Gruumush, and Knowledge to Apollo. 

The eighteen gods of Pantheon comprise the ultimate 'in' group. They are peers to each other, and no others are comparable in any way. While they certainly have their own rivalries, unless they were insane each would cooperate against any outsiders who wished to usurp their positions.
