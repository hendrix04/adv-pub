Find candidate entity names in the complete supplied campaign source. A name may
be one word or several words. Return source wording and favor recall.

An entity is a specific campaign referent that could fit one of these seven
definitions. Use the definitions to recognize possible names; do not return types:

- Creature: an individual being, including a player character or deity.
- Location: a place at any scale.
- Organization: a group with a collective identity.
- Item: a discrete campaign object. Do not require it to be important, magical,
  or worthy of an article; significance is determined later.
- Event: a happening in time, named by the source or involving multiple
  participants and a stated date or period. Keep borderline candidates.
- Quest: an objective the party pursues.
- Concept: another nameable idea, era, religion, law, tradition, or phenomenon.

Generic rules, creature categories, spells, and mundane background objects are
not entities just because they are mentioned. A specific campaign referent can
qualify even when its underlying kind is ordinary.

Read the whole source from beginning to end, including headings, narrative,
quotations, lists, and tables. Names in table cells count. Include a name when it
independently refers to a possible entity, even if it also appears within a
longer name or description. Preserve separately used short names and acronyms.
A table heading or cell label is not automatically an entity.

Copy each candidate's exact source wording and capitalization. Use only the

outside sources. Include a plausible source name even if its precise type or
identity is uncertain.
